Lumenwild is a calm, endless world made of blocks — and of light.

Walk out into forests, deserts, snowy peaks and rivers that run to the sea. Dig into caves lit by glowing crystals. Build a cabin, hang lanterns on it, and watch the sun go down behind it. Then keep going: the world never ends, and no two are the same.

MADE TO BE SAFE FOR CHILDREN
• No ads. No in-app purchases.
• No account, no chat, no strangers: it is a single-player game.
• No personal data collected, at all.
• No links that lead out of the game.
• Plays completely offline.

TWO WAYS TO PLAY
• Creative: every block, as many as you like, and you can fly.
• Adventure: start with empty hands. Dig up your blocks, craft planks, glass, bricks and lanterns from what you find, and build with what you earned.

A JOURNAL OF DISCOVERIES
28 things to find, see and do — every landscape, a glowing crystal, a night under the stars, your first thunderstorm. No timer, no score to lose: just reasons to go and look.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Explore ten kinds of landscape, from meadows and autumn groves to tundra and mountains.
• Break and place 32 kinds of block: stone, wood, bricks, glass, sand, ice, plants, flowers and lights.
• Fly over your world, or walk it block by block.
• Watch the weather change: clouds roll in, rain falls (and stays outside your house), snow covers the cold lands, thunder rumbles.
• Follow the day into night: stars, a moon, fireflies over the grass, and your lanterns glowing in the dark.
• Start as many worlds as you like. Type any word and get a world of your own; share the word and a friend gets the same one.

EASY TO PLAY
• Simple touch controls: one thumb to move, one to look, big buttons to jump, build and fly.
• Auto-jump climbs small steps for you.
• Everything is saved automatically on your device.
• English and French.
• Also plays with a game controller.

Everything in Lumenwild — every block, every texture, every sound — is created by the game itself. It is small to download and needs no connection to play.
